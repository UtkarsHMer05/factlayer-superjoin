# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated through the approved handoff: Python/FastAPI, SQLite, PyMuPDF, React/TypeScript.
## Users
Engineering assignment evaluators inspecting facts across uploaded PDFs.
## Product Purpose
Discover assertions, retain their source evidence, and explain agreement, conflict, and contextual differences.
## Operating Context
A local browser app used alongside public corporate and macroeconomic PDF reports. New PDFs must work without document-specific rules.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Upload, persisted processing, source highlights, fact search, relationship comparisons, failures, exports. Saved results must be distinguished from live processing. Unknown context and missing model access remain explicit.
## Evidence on Hand
The assignment PDF and starter archive contain six PDFs; verified demonstration candidates are documented in ANALYSIS_AND_AGENT_HANDOFF.md.
## Product Principles
Evidence before confidence. Preserve differences. Explain uncertainty. Keep source assertions immutable. Routine implementation and visual choices are delegated by the user's instruction to follow the handoff autonomously.
